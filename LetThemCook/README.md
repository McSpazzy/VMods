# Let Them Cook

Ever had someone who just can't stop walking away from the ovens, no matter how many times you tell them? Well now you can finally let them cook again.

Allows placed cooking prefabs to be permanently** flagged to never overcook food, even for players without the mod.

Ovens placed while this mod is active, will retain the ability even if the mod is deleted or disabled.

### Requirements
This requires the Unstrippable mod to also be installed on the host\server to persist through world loading. 
Vanilla client compatible if Unstrippable is installed on host\server. While they will not be able to place the fancy cookers, they will be able to use them.

### Installation
Extract the DLL from the ZIP into `[GameDirectory]\BepInEx\plugins`.
